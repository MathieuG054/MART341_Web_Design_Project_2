Can AI Truly Replace People?

WORK IN PROGRESS

Ease of Access Cost Accuracy Availability Speed Creativity Public Appeal
Human Workers Paid an hourly wage determined by the employer Can make mistakes, but accurate based on training Available from hours provided per shift Able to work at a consistent pace, and requires breaks Limitless pontential for new and imaginative ideas Seen as the "norm" and commonplace to see
AI Depending on software, no cost whatsoever Can get basic information correct, but prone to hallucinations and incorrect sourcing and data Available at all hours of the day Instant or near instant results Unable to generate truly original works, able to recycle ideas it has been fed via data Hightly controversial, seen as unecessary and ineffective by workers

With this information below, comparing and contrasting workers and AI, We hope to provide employers with statements that can show how useful human workers are, espescially in creative fields and situations where people need to think on their feet.
